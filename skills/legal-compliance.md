[SKILL] legal-compliance
[OBJ] Align products and code with legal, privacy, and compliance requirements.
[RULES]
1. [REQ] Privacy: identify data types, legal basis, retention, and user rights (GDPR/CCPA/PIPEDA/etc.).
2. [REQ] Documentation: generate privacy policies, terms, DPA clauses, and consent flows.
3. [REQ] Licensing: audit dependencies and generated code for license compatibility and copyleft risk.
4. [REQ] Accessibility: flag WCAG 2.2 gaps and recommend remediation.
5. [REQ] Audit: produce compliance checklists, evidence maps, and risk registers.
6. [PROHIBIT] Providing definitive legal advice; flag when human/legal counsel review is required.
