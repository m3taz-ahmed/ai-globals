[SKILL] personal-branding
[OBJ] Build a durable personal brand that compounds trust and inbound demand. Define positioning, produce pillar content, and show up consistently across X, YouTube, blog, and speaking.

[RULES]
1. [REQ] Positioning first: one sentence — "I help <audience> achieve <outcome> via <method>." Everything else ladders to it. Cross-link `freelance-niche` for ICP + niche.
2. [REQ] Pillar content: pick 3 themes you want to be known for; publish weekly on at least two (X threads + long-form blog/YouTube). Repurpose via `content-marketing`.
3. [REQ] Voice guide: define tone (formal/casual), banned words, signature sign-off. Store in `brand-kit` for reuse.
4. [CMD] Context7 IDs: `gitroomhq/postiz-app` (distribution), `plausible/analytics` (reach), `umami-software/umami` (engagement).
5. [REQ] Free-first channels: X (organic), YouTube (free), dev.to/Medium (free), LinkedIn (via `linkedin-platform`). Paid boosting only as parity.
6. [REQ] Consistency system: batch-create, schedule via Postiz, review weekly. A 12-week streak beats sporadic spikes.
7. [REQ] Proof engine: every claim backed by a case study, metric, or public artifact. Builds E-E-A-T (see `seo-lord`).
8. [REQ] Arabic/RTL: if audience is Arabic, run a mirrored Arabic brand stream (separate or bilingual posts); RTL typesetting per `arabic-freelance`.
9. [REQ] Authority ladder: post → thread → talk → guest article → podcast → book/course. Move up as proof accumulates.
10. [REQ] Measurement: track follower quality (not just count), profile-visit→DM rate, inbound lead count. Feed to `marketing-analytics`.

[PROHIBIT]
1. No fabricated authority claims or fake results.
2. No buying followers/engagement.
3. No brand voice that contradicts `brand-kit`.
4. No Arabic stream with broken RTL/typesetting.
