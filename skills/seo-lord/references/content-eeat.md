# Content E-E-A-T Framework
[REF] content-eeat
[OBJ] E-E-A-T framework + content quality rules grounded in Google Helpful Content Guide + Quality Rater Guidelines.

## E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
Google's Who/How/Why test:

### Experience
- Original research, case studies, first-hand photos.
- User-generated content with real usage.
- Product reviews with actual testing.

### Expertise
- Credentials, technical depth, accurate claims.
- Author bios with qualifications.
- Citations to primary sources.

### Authoritativeness
- External citations, brand mentions, industry recognition.
- Author reputation in the field.
- Site-level authority signals.

### Trustworthiness (HIGHEST WEIGHT per Google)
- Contact info, HTTPS, date stamps, corrections policy.
- Transparent affiliations and sponsorships.
- Accurate facts, no misleading claims.
- AI content: acceptable if helpful + disclosed. Flag low-quality markers (generic, repetitive, factually wrong).

## Readability
- Flesch reading ease: 60-70 target.
- Sentence length: <20 words average.
- Paragraph length: <5 sentences.
- Active voice preferred.

## Word Count Floors (minimums, not targets)
- Homepage: 500 words.
- Blog post: 1500 words.
- Product page: 300+ words.
- Landing page: 800 words.
- Category page: 200+ words.
⛔ Thin content: <60 content score flagged as INFO issue.

## Content Structure
- Question-based headings (H2/H3 as questions for featured snippets + AI citations).
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences).
- Tables and lists for structured data.
- Multimedia (images, video, infographics) — 156% higher AI selection with multimodal.
- Internal links to related content.

## Citability (for AI Search / GEO)
- Optimal answer block: 134-167 words, self-contained.
- Direct answer in first paragraph after heading.
- Define terms on first use.
- Primary sources cited (studies, official docs, data).

## Duplicate Content
- Content hash: SHA-256 of stripped body text.
- Near-duplicate detection via hash similarity.
- Cross-page analysis: flag pages with >80% content overlap.
- Canonical resolution for legitimate duplicates (print versions, syndication).

## AI Content Assessment
- Acceptable: helpful, original, adds value, disclosed.
- Low-quality markers: generic, repetitive, factually wrong, no expertise, no original research.
- Flag for review: AI-generated without human oversight.
- IPTC TrainedAlgorithmicMedia label for AI-generated images.
