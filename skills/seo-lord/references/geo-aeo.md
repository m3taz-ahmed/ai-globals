# GEO/AEO — AI Search Optimization
[REF] geo-aeo
[OBJ] AI Search Optimization (GEO/AEO) — Generative Engine Optimization aligned with Google AI Optimization Guide (2026).

## What is GEO/AEO
- AEO/GEO = rebranded SEO for AI search engines.
- AI search: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode (Gemini 2.5), Microsoft Copilot.
- Traditional SEO targets Google/Bing rankings. GEO targets AI citations + mentions.

## Citability Scoring
- Optimal answer block: 134-167 words, self-contained.
- Direct answer in first paragraph after question heading.
- Define terms on first use.
- Primary sources cited (studies, official docs, data).
- Structural readability: question-based headings, short paragraphs, tables/lists.

## Authority Signals
- Author credentials visible.
- Recency: <3 months = 3x more citable.
- Primary sources over secondary commentary.
- Original research, case studies, data.

## Multimodal Content
- Text + images/video = 156% higher AI selection.
- Infographics, charts, screenshots.
- Video transcripts for text-based AI.
- Alt text for images (AI reads alt text).

## AI Crawler Management
- GPTBot (OpenAI): Allow or Disallow via robots.txt.
- ClaudeBot (Anthropic): Allow or Disallow.
- PerplexityBot: Allow or Disallow.
- Google-Extended: Training opt-out (does not affect Search).
- Google-Agent: User-triggered, ignores robots.txt.
- CCBot (Common Crawl): Allow or Disallow.
- llms.txt: Google explicitly states it's ignored for Search rankings (primary-source: Mueller/Illyes quotes). Optional, not a ranking factor.

## Platform-Specific Insights
- AI Overviews: Ranking-correlated with organic results. Top 10 organic = likely AI Overview.
- AI Mode (Gemini 2.5): Broader pool than AI Overviews. Synthesizes multiple sources.
- ChatGPT: Wikipedia 47.9%, Reddit 11.3% of citations. Prefers authoritative + recent.
- Perplexity: Reddit 46.7% of citations. Real-time web access. Prefers structured content.
- Microsoft Copilot: Powered by Bing. Bing Places powers ChatGPT/Copilot local.

## Brand Mentions vs Backlinks
- Brand mentions correlate 3x more strongly with AI visibility than backlinks (Ahrefs study).
- Build brand awareness: PR, guest posts, social, podcasts, conferences.
- Monitor brand mentions across AI platforms.
- ChatGPT converts at 15.9% vs Google organic 1.76% (for commercial queries).
