[WORKFLOW] 53-community-building
[OBJ] Launch→moderate→grow RTL-aware community flow using `community-builder` skill and Chatwoot channel patterns for Discord/Telegram/group platforms.
[TRIGGER] community | بناء مجتمع | community building | ديسكورد | تليجرام | group growth
[RULES]
1. [REQ] Launch: choose platform(s) (Discord/Telegram/FB group) with RTL Arabic support and define community charter.
2. [REQ] Moderate: set guidelines and moderation cadence via `community-builder`; flag ToS/conduct risks.
3. [REQ] Grow: plan onboarding, events, and referral loops; hand off promo to `social-media-marketing`.
4. [REQ] Engage: draft welcome/engagement posts; require approval before publishing.
5. [REQ] Measure: track membership/engagement and feed to `marketing-analytics`.
6. [REQ] Compliance: honor platform ToS and Arabic RTL rendering.
7. [REQ] Approval gate: no publish, no outreach, no member message without explicit user approval.
[PROHIBIT]
1. No community post, outreach, or member message without explicit user approval.
2. No spam, buying members, or ToS-violating growth tactics.
3. No ToS violations on community platforms.
4. Respect marketing-compliance: opt-in for DMs, unsubscribe, GDPR on member data.
